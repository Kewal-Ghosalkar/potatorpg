## Personality
He seems like an unassuming older man, but he's disciplined, wise and a fiercely composed warrior. Despite being really observant and feeling deeply, his duty always leads him to override his feelings. Even though he is a soldier, he despises fighting and would much rather solve conflicts diplomatically.

## Main Story
### History
Born in the villages, he was a sensitive kid who often got made fun of for it which led him to start suppressing his feelings. He found it easier to avoid his feelings by working hard which makes him end up in the military. With no special skills of his own and unflashy magic, he slowly worked his way through the ranks over the decades becoming a general. Right as he is about to become the commander a young soldier named [[Fern]] surpasses him. He holds no resentment towards Fern however, as it only motivates him to work harder. He often found himself sparring with Fern and they slowly get closer which makes him see Fern almost like his own daughter. Often acts like Fern's advisor, helping her with everything from military strategy to her brother. Slowly he also learns about the King's past which leads him to figure out that the player and the King are the same thing.
### [[Act I]]
Fern leaves him to defend the rear as she charges forward. While he hates to see her go alone, he is duty bound and holds back. Later watching you approach him, he realizes that Fern has been killed. While devastated internally, he is aware that he cannot stop you and his duty is to try to negotiate. Unfortunately because of the [[Old Potato]]'s manipulation the player ends up killing his subordinates which is the breaking point. The general lashes out with all his fury and this is an incredibly difficult fight. 
As you fight, the general looses pieces of his leaves which get more battered. However when you finish him, his secret ability activates which allows him to revive as long as his core is intact. He revives off-screen.
### [[Act II (True-Pacifist)]]
He follows you secretly though the villages which makes him question your morality. 
Off-screen when he sees [[Clover]] leave behind [[Onion Child]], he consoles him. Instead of asking [[Onion Child]] to act strong, he reminds him that "courage is not the absence of fear". This is the first time the General realizes that maybe he's allowed to listen to his own feelings.
He shows up again right as Clover is rushing in to take his revenge. Unable to bear watching clover repeat Fern's actions, he jumps in and grabs Clover before a fight can start. As Clover tries to struggle out of his arms, he asks "Would Fern want this?", which calms Clover as they both escape. 
He reminds Clover that he hurt [[Onion Child]] by rushing in. Later as the villagers surround you, the general and Clover watch from afar until he notices that Clover jumped in the fray. Watching Clover help you, he follows closely behind watching you interact with Clover. Seeing as you don't harm him, the general faces you next. The [[Conflicted General]] [[TBD]] fight begins.
This is a relatively easy fight as his attacks are hesitant. He explains his backstory and how he's torn on what to feel about you. He mentions how both Clover and [[Onion Child]] both grew up to give you a chance, and how he also wants to believe in you. The fight ends when he is convinced to give you a chance.
He shows up in the final fight against the Old Potato as he uses his magic to improve your focus while also disorienting the old potato. 
### [[Act II (Genocide)]]
Right as he revives and reaches the villages, he sees you about to kill Clover. Overcome with emotion he stands in the way, tanking the finishing blow. The [[One more second]] fight begins.
At the brink of death the general asks Clover to run and evacuate the villages, and that he will buy some time. Facing you, the General becomes unnervingly composed as he takes up his sword fighting stance. The general says nothing for the rest of the fight as a terrifyingly technical battle begins. No matter how much damage he takes his stance doesn't falter, no matter how much his leaves tear off he keeps standing. Right as you land the finishing blow, his stance finally breaks, right then he says, "Not Yet" as he gets back into his stance with 1 HP for his final attack.
