## Summary
### Intro
You start in a dark expanse, suddenly a ray of light shines above you. You reach out for it and burst out of the ground, Standing next to you, the old potato explains that this is a world of vegetables. You are the special hero of this world, you are a Potato. As a potato you have immense power including the ability to resurrect through a piece of you buried in the ground. You are destined to defeat the last hero of this world, who abused his immense power to overthrow the vegetable king. With old potato as you teacher, the two of you set out to journey to the castle.

### Military Camps
You journey through the military camps as you defeat the kings army. You level up as you gain xp in every fight and slowly unlock better weapons and new magic.

### Castle
You face the strongest of the king's army until you reach the throne room. You call out to the evil king. The king faces you as the reality dawns on you, the king is you. The old man screams that it's just one of his evil tricks. The final battle begins.

### Final Battle
After a desperate struggle, you and the king both drop to their knees. Exhausted and on 1 HP, the king asks if they can just talk. Right before you can accept/reject his proposal, a weapon slashes through the king. Its the old potato. The old potato laughs maniacally as he thanks you levelling him up enough to dispose of that fool. The true final battle begins.

### True Final Battle
Its you vs the old potato, but suddenly you find that your level is back to 1 and you have none of the magic you unlocked. With your weapon in hand you struggle desperately as the old man uses all the spells you unlocked against you and slashes you down.

### The End?
The world goes black. Are you dead? Suddenly a ray of light shines above you. You reach out for it and burst out of the ground. You are back to the start, however there is no old potato standing over you.
