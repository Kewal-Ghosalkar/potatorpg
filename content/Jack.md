## Personality
Muscular gym-bro with an uplifting personality. Loves to work his arms but always skips leg day. Loves encouraging everyone and helping around camp. Massive dumbass and says "BRO" a lot.

## Main Story
### History
Grew up in [[First Village]], he was an active kid that played all day. One day he met a frill kid named [[Jill]]. He asks "Hey BRO, you liftin that?" pointing to Jill's table. Jack and Jill end up best friends and gym-buds. Slowly as they grow into adults, Jack starts harboring feelings towards Jill, however decides to keep this a secret out of a fear of ruining their gym-bro-ness. He is drafted into the military because of his healthy body. He is sad to leave Jill but promises to return soon. Jack and Jill both exchange letters every week during his deployment.
### [[Act I]]
Jack is a recurring character you run into as you defeat the king's army. His adorable dumb-assary allows you to get out of a trap set up to stop you. Eventually you fight his subordinate (Who he just calls BRO) who is buffed by Jack's magic. Jack cheers from the background. After defeating the subordinate, Jack faces you. Jack uses his magic to turn into *Jack the Jacked* as his physical attacks grow faster. Jack is uncharacteristically quiet during this fight. As you land the finishing blow Jack says "Jill....".
