## Main Story
### History
Jill was born into a very frail body in the [[First Village]]. He was often bullied for his frail body which made him socially anxious. Even as a young child Jill had resigned on ever having a fulfilling life; until he meets [[Jack]]. After Jack makes him laugh, the two become inseparable as Jack encourages Jill to work out to improve his body. As they grow up, Jill starts having complicated feelings about Jack, however before he can bring it up, Jack gets drafted into the military. They share letters every week as Jill grows a little distant again, however this time he has hope in the promise Jack made to return soon.
