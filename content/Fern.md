## Personality
Outwards: An elegant, strong, responsible leader. Stern, unyielding and can even be scary. Calculating but dives head first to protect her fellow soldiers. 
Inwards: Messy, constantly questions her decisions , cares way too much about the soldiers. Dotes on her younger brother [[Clover]] and is always worried about him. Humble enough to ask the [[General]] for advice about strategy, leadership and her brother.
## Main Story
### History
Born in the [[Second Village]], Fern was already a sword fighting prodigy from a very young age. She joined the army and quickly rose up the ranks with her skills, becoming the youngest general in military history, beating out older and more experienced generals with pure skill.

### [[Act I]]
Commander fern is introduced through the various soldiers that you fight. She looms over as a threat to you as someone who would strike you down.
Watching you kill her soldiers, Fern is unable to sit and watch. She Jumps out, leaving the [[General]] in-charge to defend the rear. As you are about to kill a soldiesr [[tbd]], Fern jumps in blocking the finishing blow as the [[Fern the Flash]] fight begins. 
It's an incredibly difficult fight where Fern gets faster with every attack.
